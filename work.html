Marketing DirectorCurrent

Aurangabad Pipes Pvt. Ltd. · Nov 2026 to present · Full-time · On-site, Aurangabad, Maharashtra

Problem
Take a seamless tube maker with more than 25 years in the industry to buyers who have not heard of it yet, in India and abroad.
Context

Aurangabad Pipes makes hot-finished and cold-drawn seamless steel tubes and pipes in carbon and alloy steel.

Its tubes go into boilers, heat exchangers, process piping, hydraulic cylinders, automobiles, machines and steel structures.

The company is ISO 9001:2015, IBR and PED certified. Every tube starts as a billet on its own hot mill, is checked in its own lab with eddy current and hydro tests, and carries a heat number for traceability.

My role
  • Marketing and new business, domestic and international.
  • Making the brand look as good as the product already is, so OEMs, EPC projects and plants can find and trust it.
Result or target
Started in November 2026. On 4 November 2026 the company website, aurangabadpipes.com, went live, a team effort I announced on LinkedIn. Commercial results will be added here as they are earned. New role: no results claimed yet

Approach

How I approach a problem

  1. Understand the real problem

    I start by listening closely and separating signal from noise, so the real challenge becomes clear.

  2. Define the sharp move

    Whether it is positioning, go-to-market or a system change, I focus people on the action that creates leverage.

  3. Build what lasts

    Good decisions scale when the process behind them is simple, repeatable and aligned with the bigger goal.

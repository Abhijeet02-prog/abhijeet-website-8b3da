My story

Creative mind. Commercial instinct.

Raised in manufacturing. Trained in money.

I grew up in a business environment, around Disha Auto Components and Aurangabad Pipes, and trained as a mechanical engineer at VIT. My first wins were on the factory floor at Disha: automating a production step and redesigning paint curing from 8 hours to 10 minutes.

A PGDM in marketing and finance at Goa Institute of Management took me into private banking at ICICI, working with some of India’s wealthiest families. Today I build growth engines, with one rule from both worlds: clarity before scale.

Along the way: Senior Core Committee at OxyGIM, a blood-donation camp with 500+ volunteers, and outreach to 700+ residents across five mining-affected villages in Goa.

Story

My story

I'm an engineer who became a banker who became a marketer. Somewhere along the way I realised I'm most useful when I'm joining dots that other people treat as separate.

I grew up in a business environment, close to manufacturing companies such as Disha Auto Components and Aurangabad Pipes. That is where I first saw how a shop floor, a balance sheet and long-standing customer relationships hold a company together.

I studied mechanical engineering at VIT Vellore, then marketing and finance at Goa Institute of Management. Then came ICICI Bank in Kolkata, as a Family Office Specialist across 28 branches in the Alipore Zone, looking after the money of families who don't hand out trust easily.

Today I'm the Marketing Director at Aurangabad Pipes Pvt. Ltd. We make seamless steel tubes and pipes, hot-finished and cold-drawn, in carbon and alloy steel. My job is easy to say and hard to do: get more people in India and abroad to know us, trust us and buy from us. On the side, I help EdgeX Academy grow, mostly by building campus partnerships.

Most days I write about oil, gold, IPOs, the RBI, banking, crypto, geopolitics and, now and then, sport. People ask why I don't pick a lane. I don't see separate lanes. When a tanker gets hit near Hormuz, it shows up in the rupee, then in inflation, then in what a factory pays for its inputs. I like tracing that chain and putting it in plain words.

Different cities, different rooms, one lesson: people buy from people they trust.

ICICI: the CEO factory of India?

By Abhijeet ToshniwalFirst published on LinkedIn, 2 October 2026

HDFC Bank's incoming MD and CEO Anup Bagchi has spent 34 years in the ICICI Group. And then I started looking at where some of India's other financial-sector leaders came from. The pattern is difficult to ignore.

And these aren't merely employees who happened to move companies. Many were given exposure across retail banking, corporate banking, investment banking, insurance, asset management, international banking, credit and P&L leadership relatively early in their careers.

There may be another reason too. Former ICICI chairman N. Vaghul reportedly had ICICI send appointment letters directly to the top 20 rankers in the Chartered Accountancy exams, sometimes without even interviewing them.

Maybe that's the real story. Hire exceptional people. Give them responsibility. Rotate them across businesses. Let them build. And eventually, some of them build institutions themselves.

ICICI didn't just build businesses across Indian financial services. It helped build a generation of people who would go on to lead them. And with Anup Bagchi now moving to HDFC Bank, perhaps the phrase “ICICI, India's CEO factory” has never looked more relevant.

Tenures are approximate in cases where publicly available biographies provide years rather than exact joining or leaving dates.

About

I’m an engineer who became a banker who became a marketer. Somewhere along the way I realised I’m most useful when I’m joining dots that other people treat as separate.

Right now I’m the Marketing Director at Aurangabad Pipes Pvt. Ltd. We’ve been making seamless steel tubes and pipes for over 25 years, hot-finished and cold-drawn, in carbon and alloy steel. My job is easy to say and hard to do: get more people in India and abroad to know us, trust us and buy from us.

My path here wasn’t a straight line. I started in engineering, then moved to ICICI Bank in Kolkata as a Family Office Specialist across 28 branches in the Alipore Zone, looking after the money of families who don’t hand out trust easily. On the side, I help EdgeX Academy grow, mostly by building campus partnerships.

I studied mechanical engineering at VIT Vellore, then marketing and finance at Goa Institute of Management.
